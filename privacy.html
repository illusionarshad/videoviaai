Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

VideoViaAI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website and use our AI image and video generation services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your name and email address to manage your subscription and generation history.
  • Prompts & Generated Content: Text prompts you enter are sent to third-party AI providers solely to generate your requested images or videos.
  • Browser Storage & Usage Limits: We use browser local storage and cookies to track free daily generation allowances (2 images and 1 video per day) and remember your preferences.
  • Contact Form Messages: Information submitted through our contact form is used only to respond to your inquiry.

2. Advertising & Google AdSense

Our free tier is supported by advertisements, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Payments

Paid subscriptions are processed securely through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay and PayPal. VideoViaAI does not store your full credit card, debit card, or banking details on our servers.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall website traffic and improve performance. These tools collect anonymized usage data such as page views, device type, and general region.

5. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Free-tier generations are not permanently stored. Paid-tier history is retained according to your active plan (7 days, 30 days, or forever while active).

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us through our Contact page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our platform grows. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
